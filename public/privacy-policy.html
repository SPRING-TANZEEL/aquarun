💧 AquaRun — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

AquaRun ("we", "our", or "us") is a water delivery management software operated by Muhammad Tanzeel Ur Rehman, based in Kamoke, Pakistan. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application at aquarun.pk.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide our services:

  • Business information: Business name, contact details, and settings
  • Customer data: Customer names, addresses, phone numbers, and delivery history
  • Rider data: Rider names, GPS location during active deliveries, and delivery records
  • Transaction data: Orders, deliveries, payments, and financial records
  • Device data: Device type and browser information for app optimization

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide water delivery management services
  • To track deliveries and rider locations during working hours
  • To generate invoices, reports, and financial records
  • To send push notifications about orders and deliveries
  • To improve our services

3. GPS / Location Data

AquaRun collects rider GPS location data only during active delivery sessions to enable live tracking for business administrators. Location data is not collected when the rider app is closed or when the rider is not on duty. Location data is stored securely and is only accessible to the business owner/admin.

4. Data Storage

All data is stored securely on Supabase cloud infrastructure with encryption. Data is stored in compliance with industry security standards. Each business tenant's data is isolated and only accessible by authorized users of that business.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties except:

  • Supabase (our database provider) for data storage
  • Vercel (our hosting provider) for app delivery
  • Google Maps API for location services
  • When required by law

6. Data Retention

Business data is retained for as long as the account is active. Upon account termination, data may be deleted upon request. Transaction records may be retained for accounting purposes as required by Pakistani business law.

7. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), secure authentication, and access controls to protect your information.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data

9. Children's Privacy

AquaRun is a business management tool and is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes via the application.
